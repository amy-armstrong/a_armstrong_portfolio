CECI

PROJECT OVERVIEW

CECI is an elegant makeup brand designed with the idea of timeless beauty for women, created by women. The project focused on branding, visual identity, and packaging design to convey sophistication and inclusivity.

TIMEFRAME OCT-NOV 2025
MY ROLE Graphic Designer Motion Designer Researcher
TOOLS Illustrator Photoshop After Effects

THE CHALLENGE

Creating a luxury makeup brand for women that feels both timeless and inclusive required balancing elegance with accessibility. The challenge was designing a visual identity that communicates sophistication without alienating a broad audience.

OBJECTIVE / GOAL

The goal was to develop a cohesive brand identity, from logo and typography to packaging, that conveys refinement and confidence. The result is a visually elegant and approachable brand that resonates with its audience.

ZIMA

PROJECT OVERVIEW

ZIMA is a nostalgic rebrand of the iconic 1980s beverage, refreshed for a modern audience. The project focused on creating a playful yet sleek visual identity, packaging, and promotional materials that capture the drink’s cool, retro vibe.

TIMEFRAME MAY-AUG 2025
MY ROLE UI/UX Designer Motion Designer Developer Researcher
TOOLS Figma Photoshop Cinema 4D Illustrator VS Code After Effects GitHub

THE CHALLENGE

Reintroducing a nostalgic beverage from the 1980s meant appealing to both original fans and a new generation. The challenge was modernizing the brand while retaining the playful, retro charm that makes ZIMA iconic.

OBJECTIVE / GOAL

The objective was to create a vibrant visual identity, packaging, and promotional materials that capture ZIMA’s fun, refreshing personality. The outcome is a modern rebrand that is recognizable, engaging, and market-ready.
