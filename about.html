HI THERE! I'M AMY

ABOUT
ME

I’m a 23 year old Filipino-Canadian visual designer based in the Greater Toronto Area, currently finishing my studies in Interactive Media Design at Fanshawe College in London, Ontario. My work focuses on branding and product design, with a strong emphasis on UX/UI—creating thoughtful, user-centered experiences that balance aesthetics and functionality. I enjoy bringing ideas to life through clean, intentional design that solves real problems. Outside of work, I like to stay active and social. You’ll usually find me at the gym, attempting new PRs, or spending time with friends and family. I also enjoy exploring new places, pushing myself to new limits, keeping up with design trends for inspiration, and occasionally diving into creative side projects that let me experiment with new styles and ideas.
